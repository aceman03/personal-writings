# Book One: The Last Dawn — Timeline

> **SPOILERS.** Author's planning document. Not linked from the website.
> Day 0 is the last ordinary day. Day 1 is the Silence. The climax falls on Day 19.
> Updated after each chapter. Chapters in brackets.

## Day 0 — the last ordinary day
- Solmere: Corvel's Reading (E-class truth-sense, a Thalen blessing). [1]
- Halvenreach: Edda finds her bottomless well; Ilsabet sees the Thornwall *holds* something. [1]
- The Wildlands go silent; Hask feels the wall's hum. Night: Vessa and Ryssa under the Steppe stars. [1, 8]

## Day 1 — the Silence
- **Dawn:** the Bright Court goes silent; the Thornwall goes dark; Karrog's blessings blaze up; the Nameless One laughs. [1, 2, 8]
- Greywatch: Aldric loses the Shield of Dawn and evacuates ~300 civilians and 40 soldiers south; Nyx's bargain (*"nobody puts me on a pyre"*). [2]
- Solmere: the king kneels and won't speak; the Chancellor's order for all mages goes south before the bells stop. [4]
- Steppe: Vessa saves Edric Saltby. [8]
- **Night:** skirls attack the Lantern House; two soldiers fall; Pip lights gold; the giant rises beyond the wall and turns toward the light. Urzag and Karrog feel the gold. [2, 3, 8]

## Day 2
- The column flees down the Pale Road with the giant behind (one step every 110 heartbeats); Pip wakes (*"Sorry"*). [3]
- **Noon:** the footsteps stop. *"It's waiting."* [6]
- Solmere: Rhoan rides east by the Kingsway with the royal army; Corvel sits council. Steppe: the Red Fire; Ryssa takes the red hand. [4, 8]

## Day 3
- Greywatch reaches Fort Harrow (about 340 souls; 3,000 refugees already there). Toman posted to the north gate. Nyx jailed that night. [6]
- Solmere: Corvel sees Greaves lie. Halvenreach: the order is dated 23 days before the Silence. Steppe: Urzag's war council. [4, 5, 8]

## Day 4
- **Dawn:** Hask sees three giants (six blue eyes) 10–12 miles north. [6]
- Nyx released; the camp crosses to the south bank; Brokk arrives; the first bridge ward relit that night; *"the first writing was carved to hold the gods to their word."* [7]
- Steppe: Urzag's 200 riders head north-west. [8]

## Day 5
- Ansel Rooke and Tamsin Reed answer Kell's call; four wards lit; Brother Fen begins preaching. Steppe: the raid on Gorse End. [8, 9]

## Day 6
- All seven wards lit by midnight. Night: Ryssa and fourteen others burn out; Vessa paints the red hand. [8, 9]

## Day 7
- **Dusk:** Urzag's riders (about 185) light red fires on the north ridge.
- **Night:** the three giants drive the skirls onto the bridge; Aldric takes command from a frozen Kell; Toman dies at the stair; Pip lights gold on the tower roof; the giant breaks the bridge (four of seven arches, the center tower). Losses: Toman, two of Brokk's kin, seven Greywatch men, 100+ of Kell's garrison. [9]

## Day 8
- **Before dawn:** the giant stands in the river and waits; Vessa watches Pip from the north bank. [9]
- Morning: the red fires leave the north ridge, riding east; at dusk Hask reports it and Collum names Wyck's Ford. The food stores are lost. [10]

## Day 9
- Small hours: Pip wakes (*"somebody who needs me"*). Noon: Fen's sermon blames the witches' wards. [10]

## Day 10
- Morning: the mob and the pyre; Aldric keeps his word. Noon: Bram, Fen, 22 Greywatch men and ~1,000 refugees leave south for Solmere.
- Before dusk: Pip is gone, barefoot, east toward Wyck's Ford and a red fire. [10]

## Ahead
- **Days 10–11:** The Choice (the orc camp; Vessa frees Pip). [11]
- **Day 19:** the High Dawn rite at the Sunspire.
