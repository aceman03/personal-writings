# Book One: The Last Dawn — Chapter Outline

> **SPOILERS.** Author's planning document. Not linked from the website.
> See `series-plan.md` for the hidden truth behind these events.

22 chapters in four parts, ~120,000 words (~440 printed pages).
Target 15–20 pages per chapter; every chapter ends on a cliffhanger.

**The shape of the book:** a giant follows Pip's gold light south, so the party's flight
becomes a race to the capital. Two side threads (Solmere's court and the Collegium) run
alongside and converge with the party at the Sunspire for the climax.

**Timeline:** Day 0 is the last ordinary day. Day 1 is the Silence. The climax falls on Day 19,
the first dawn of the new month, when the Dawnwardens traditionally hold the High Dawn rite.

## Part One: The Silence

### 1. The World That Was ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Many (Oren, Edda, Hale, Ilsabet, Tyriel, Hagra, Urzag, Hask, Sereth) · **Day 0 → dawn of Day 1**
The last ordinary day across Caelmar. Corvel graded E for truth-sense; Edda finds a bottomless
well; Ilsabet realizes the Thornwall *holds*; the Wildlands go silent.
**Cliffhanger:** The Bright Court goes silent, the Thornwall goes dark, and the Nameless One laughs.

### 2. Greywatch ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Aldric, Nyx · **Day 1**
Aldric loses the Shield of Dawn. Greywatch flees south. Nyx charges five of six wardstones.
Skirls attack the Lantern House; Nyx runs dry and bloodcasting fails; Pip lights a gold circle
and Aldric feels Aurem for a heartbeat.
**Cliffhanger:** A giant rises beyond the wall, with Thornwall-blue eyes, and turns toward the gold light.

### 3. The Long Night ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Aldric · **Night of Day 1 → Day 2**
- The column flees the Lantern House in the dark, in silence, with the footsteps behind them.
- Pip won't wake. Dagny refuses to let anyone near her; Brother Fen calls her a miracle; Collum calls her a curse.
- Aldric must decide what Pip is. He keeps her with the column under his own protection.
- Nyx, wrecked from the failed bloodcast, can't cast at all. The first honest conversation between her and Aldric.
- Hask works out that the giant is slow but never stops, and it is following the column's path exactly.
- Dawn of Day 2: no Aurem again. Aldric doesn't kneel this time.
**Cliffhanger:** Pip wakes, looks north, and says calmly: *"It isn't following us. It's following me."*

### 4. The Empty Throne ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Corvel, Oren · **Days 1–3, Solmere**
- Corvel's truth-sense is a Thalen blessing, so it goes dark with the rest. For the first time in his life, he can't tell when people lie.
- Solmere in panic: crowds at the temples, the Houses of Mending closed, a run on the hedge-mages.
- King Haldren, A-class (the Crown of Dawn), kneels in the royal chapel and won't speak. Crown Prince Rhoan rides north with the Lord Marshal; Corvel must sit council as the prince of the blood.
- Oren, appointed his tutor, shows him that twelve years of tasting lies taught him what they *look* like: *a blessing is what you do with it.* Advice: don't tell the council the gift is gone; careful people are easier to watch.
- Mother Wrenna of Vhessane offers the Pale Grace in the river market; it cures, and leaves chalk-white marks.
- The council votes to tolerate the Hollow Choir preachers. Magus Quill notes the Chancellor's order for all mages went south before the bells stopped ringing.
**Cliffhanger:** Without any blessing, Corvel reads it in Greaves's face: the Chancellor is lying. He knew.

### 5. The Unlocked Door ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Edda, Ilsabet · **Days 2–3, Halvenreach**
*As written:* crowds on the Collegium bridges and a gouged price for mendings; Hale's sister Agnes, a healer
who lost Lyssa; Archmagus Ottilie Brandt unlocks Ilsabet's door and trades her the Sealed Archive key and the
Thornwall records; Ilsabet shows Hale the rubbing and he asks her to keep it quiet; Edda's Light of the
Lantern-Keeper sets the practice room on fire, and Ilsabet fixes it with hair-thin conduits; Lark Ellery
(the girl from the third row) is noticed; the relay rider arrives; the order says *the Silence* and its
registry stamp is dated twenty-three days before it.
- The Collegium is suddenly the most important building in the Hearthlands. The crown requisitions every mage.
- Edda is rushed through training because her well is deep. Hale is shaken but steady.
- The Archmagus unlocks Ilsabet's door for the first time in nineteen years and asks for help.
- Ilsabet shows Hale the rubbing. He doesn't want to believe her.
- Edda overloads her first real spell and nearly burns down a classroom: the danger of a deep well without control.
- A royal courier arrives with a sealed order: all spellcrafters to Solmere.
**Cliffhanger:** Ilsabet reads the order and realizes it was written *before* the Silence. Someone knew they would be needed.

## Part Two: The Road South

### 6. Fort Harrow ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Aldric, Nyx, Hask · **Day 2 noon → dawn of Day 4**
*As written:* the footsteps stop at noon on Day 2 (*"It's waiting"*); Pip chooses to stay with Aldric, Nyx and
Dagny, and describes a white tower full of gold (the Sunspire); Fort Harrow is packed with survivors of the
Thornwall towns (Thane's Cross lost everyone in its god-warded temple); Lord-Commander Ressa Kell, who lost
the Unbroken Line, absorbs Aldric's garrison, refuses to evacuate, and learns nothing about Pip (Aldric keeps
it from her); the royal army went east by the Kingsway and never came; Toman is posted to the north gate;
Pip tells Nyx the center glyph *"is somebody"*; Nyx is jailed under Kell's misreading of the royal order and
doesn't break out; Aldric frees her through the law and Magistrate Pell, apologizing publicly; dwarves are
coming down the passes (*"the stone is humming the wrong way"*).
**Cliffhanger (as written):** Hask sees three pairs of Thornwall-blue eyes over the northern hills. It stopped to wait for the others.
- The column reaches Fort Harrow on the bridge over the river Grey. It is not a refuge: overcrowded with refugees from every Thornwall settlement, under a commander (Lord-Commander Ressa Kell, blessed by Thalen) who has lost her nerve along with her blessing.
- Aldric argues for evacuating further south; Kell refuses and claims command of his soldiers.
- Nyx is taken into custody as an "unlicensed mage." Aldric keeps his word and gets her out.
- News comes from the Kharn passes: dwarves are coming down out of the mountains.
**Cliffhanger:** Hask, watching the north from the bridge, sees the giant's blue eyes on the horizon. It is closer than yesterday, and it is not alone.

### 7. Iron and Oath ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Brokk, Aldric, Nyx · **Day 4 and the night after**
*As written:* Brokk's opening: stone has always hummed downward, and on the morning of the Silence it turned
upward; the Root Halls under Kharn-Ostra grew warm; Hagra (*"When the stone hums toward you, something is coming
up"*) leads the Stonebrow and Ironmantle clans down and sends Brokk to Fort Harrow. Kell moves the camp across
the river and refuses Nyx's help in front of her captains; Aldric explains why. A child named Harrow is born
under the tower. The camp mutters *mountain-rats*. Pip: *"He can hear it too."* Kell's axe is Hagra's gift after
the nine-day siege. Brokk will relay the dead bridge wards for valley land south of the Grey (*"My king isn't
here"*). Brokk and Nyx clash over craft versus magic, then work through the night; she discovers he's been
feeding mana into every cut all his life. Hask and Brokk compare what stone feels like. Brokk sees Pip's circle
in Nyx's notebook: beneath the Vaelic in the Root Halls lies an older, curved writing, warm since the Silence.
- Introduce **Brokk Ironmantle**, a dwarven forge-master who led a column of his clan down from the Kharn.
- The dwarves weren't driven out by the Silence (few of them were blessed). Something is waking *under* the mountains: deep tunnels warming, stone humming the wrong way.
- Brokk and Nyx clash, then bond over craft versus magic. He recognizes Hagra Stonebrow's maker's mark on an axe and hates that Nyx calls it spellcraft.
- Brokk sees Pip's circle drawn in a notebook and goes very quiet. Dwarven legend has a word for curved, angle-less glyphs: "the first writing."
**Cliffhanger:** Brokk says the old dwarven saying out loud: *"The first writing was carved to hold the gods to their word."*

### 8. The Red Hand ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Vessa · **Day 0 night → dawn of Day 7, the Ashen Steppe to the Hearthlands border**
*As written:* Vessa (22, chosen by Karrog two summers ago) and her sister Ryssa (16) under the stars; at dawn
Karrog's blessing blazes up and the border's sun-shrines go dark; Vessa saves the salt trader Edric Saltby
from her own clan; at the Red Fire on the Anvil, Gorrath the Burned gives Karrog's red hand to anyone who
will *"go where Karrog sends"*; Grandmother Ulla's drought story (moral gray: *"You tell me which one I should
be grateful to"*); Ryssa takes the red hand; Urzag's war council: Karrog felt the gold and wants its maker
alive; two hundred riders head north-west; Marrak (*"the best war any of us will ever see"*); at Gorse End the
newly blessed raid against orders, Ryssa doesn't recognize Vessa, Urzag stops them with Karrog's word; Ryssa
burns out from the inside (*"He's a fire. And I'm the wood."*). Vessa paints the red hand on her face with
her dead sister's hand.
**Cliffhanger (as written):** Urzag's map: the gold is heading for Fort Harrow, and they'll be there by dusk. He's afraid: something older than Karrog felt the light too, and it's walking toward the bridge.
- Introduce **Vessa**, a young orc champion of Karrog. Her blessing still works and is stronger than ever.
- On the Steppe, Karrog is offering blessings freely. Urzag gathers a warband of the newly blessed.
- Urzag's orders: ride north fast, scout the Hearthlands' collapse, and bring back "the one who lit the gold." Karrog wants Pip.
- Vessa rides with them. She sees what Karrog's new blessings do to people who take them carelessly.
**Cliffhanger:** Urzag tells her where they're going: Fort Harrow. Karrog has felt the gold light, and so has something older.

### 9. The Siege of the Bridge ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Nyx, Aldric · **Day 5 → dawn of Day 8**
*As written:* Kell's crier calls for anyone with a circle; two come: Ansel Rooke (harness-mender, 41 years of a
secret mending circle) and Tamsin Reed (laundress, a wash-water circle and a deep well). Nyx teaches them to feel
their wells; Brokk cuts feed-stones in the killing passage. All seven wards lit by midnight of Day 6. Brother
Fen begins preaching that the witches cut the Warden's name out of the gate (the pyre's wood is chosen). Dusk
of Day 7: Urzag's riders light red fires on the north ridge and wait. All three giants advance, driving the
skirls ahead of them like beaters; the skirls flow around the orcs' hill. The wards burn the first waves; skirls
climb the piers. Aldric fights without Aurem and finds the drills were always his. Two giants stop on the valley
floor; the one from the Lantern House comes on alone. Kell freezes (*"It will hold"*); Aldric gives the orders
and Captain Dunmore obeys. Tamsin nearly burns out on a feed-stone; Nyx chooses to stop. Sir Toman holds the
stair foot so the others can carry Tamsin out (*"Steady"*). Pip lights gold on the tower roof: the skirls flee,
Aldric feels Aurem for one heartbeat, the giant walks through the gold and reaches for Pip, and its hand breaks
the bridge. Four of seven arches fall, the center tower with them. Losses: Toman, two of Brokk's kin, seven of
Greywatch's thirty-eight, more than a hundred of Kell's garrison; some cut off on the north bank. The giant
stops in the river and waits again.
**Cliffhanger (as written):** At first light, across the gap, a lone orc rider with a small red handprint over her left cheek and eye watches, not the giant, but Pip.
- Night assault on Fort Harrow: skirls from the north, the giant behind them.
- Aldric fights without a god and leads without permission when Kell freezes.
- Nyx, Brokk and the fort's two hedge-mages hold the bridge wards together.
- Sir Toman dies holding the north gate, his "steady hands" on a crossbow to the last.
- Pip lights gold again. It drives the skirls back, but the giant doesn't stop this time. It reaches the river.
- The bridge falls.
**Cliffhanger:** Across the broken bridge, in the giant's blue light, Aldric sees a rider in red watching: an orc with a red hand painted on her face.

### 10. Grubbers ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Nyx · **Dawn of Day 8 → dusk of Day 10**
*As written:* No one kneels at dawn. The fort's food stores went into the Grey with the arches; the first fights
over food; Pell's ration line runs out; Kell sits silent and her captains report to Aldric. The red fires leave
the ridge, and Collum names Wyck's Ford, twelve miles east, where the orcs crossed nine years ago. Pip wakes:
she knew the giant would come for her (*"The voice said to"*), and the voice now says *"there's somebody who
needs me."* Fen's sermon: the giant came for the witches' blue, not the gold. He's half right and protecting
Pip without knowing it. Nyx could save herself by telling the truth about Pip, and doesn't. Day 10: the mob
takes Tamsin, beats Ansel, seizes Nyx; Brokk and four kin stand; Sir Bram stands with Fen. Aldric keeps his word
*"in front of whoever you like"* (*"I swore an oath to Aurem. I gave my word to her. One of them is still here
to hold me to it."*). Bram leads 22 of the 31 Greywatch men and about a thousand refugees south, with Fen. Meg
Ashby and Rosie stand in front of the pyre first, then Dagny, Pell, Hask, Dunmore and the nine; the mob melts.
**Cliffhanger (as written):** Pip is gone. Her bare footprints run straight east along the river toward Wyck's Ford, where a red fire is burning.
- The survivors are scattered south of the river. Panic turns ugly.
- Brother Fen preaches that mages caused the Silence; the refugees turn on Nyx, Brokk and the fort's hedge-mages.
- A mob builds a pyre, exactly as Nyx predicted.
- Aldric stands in front of it and keeps his word in front of everyone. It costs him the refugees' trust and most of his remaining soldiers.
- Nyx sees, for the first time, what the bargain cost him.
**Cliffhanger:** In the confusion, Pip is gone. Her tracks lead toward the orc camp.

### 11. The Choice ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Aldric · **Dusk of Day 10 → dawn of Day 11**
*As written:* Aldric, Nyx, Hask and Brokk go on foot; Dagny is held back (*"You bring her back"*); Kell nods.
Pip's prints walk straight in among six scouts' horses: she went to them. On the march, Brokk names his two dead
(Orri and Bekka Stonebrow), explains that dwarves raise their cairns young (Hagra eats lunch on hers), and
wonders what a word is worth if the first writing was needed to hold the gods to theirs; Nyx says *"Thank you"*
and forbids discussion. Wyck's Mill: red fires, the newly blessed sleepless with red-ember eyes, Pip tied to
the millstone at Urzag's feet. Plan: Hask drives the horses, Nyx sets a fire, Brokk covers the ford. Aldric
wades in and Vessa catches him; she tells him Pip walked up to Marrak's horse and said *hello*; 23 of the
newly blessed have burned out; the red hand is her sister's. Nyx's fire goes up too early; Vessa calls the
champions and Aldric is taken. Nyx, Hask and Brokk escape into the river. Urzag: *"If my god doesn't have her,
that will. Which do you think is kinder?"* Tied with Pip, Aldric learns the voice speaks through the center
glyph and *"says sorry, mostly."* Pip to Vessa: *"You're holding a wolf by the scruff. All night. Every night.
And it's pulling."*
**Cliffhanger (as written):** Vessa cuts their bonds, flinches as Karrog's brand burns between her shoulders, and whispers: *"Run. I'm coming with you."*
- Aldric, Nyx, Hask and Brokk track Pip toward Urzag's camp.
- They find that Pip walked there herself, because "the voice said to."
- Infiltration of the orc camp. Vessa finds them first.
- Vessa has been ordered to hand Pip to Urzag for Karrog. She has watched Karrog's blessings eat people alive for six days.
**Cliffhanger:** Vessa cuts Pip's bonds and says: *"Run. I'm coming with you."* Her blessing burns like a brand as she says it.

## Part Three: Convergence

### 12. Vessa's Bargain ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Vessa, Aldric · **Dawn of Day 11 → night of Day 12**
*As written:* Karrog's brand burns once and then stops, and Vessa hears him laugh. Down the wheel-race into the
Grey; Nyx, Hask and Brokk waited in the willows all night. Vessa kills two of the newly blessed (Daro of the
Kesh) in the alders. The gorse ravine. At noon Marrak and eight champions block the cleft (*"Urzag says come
home"*); Vessa breaks three of them, and Karrog says *"Yes. Good. Again."*; she spares Marrak. Aldric sees the face
of the boy at the mill on her, and that she stopped; Brokk: *"Glad she's on our side. Today."* The leash
explained: power flows while you go where you're pulled; a champion of the Ossa took a day to die. Nyx sees
herself in Vessa (*"Waiting to see what you'll do"*). Day 12 dawn: they can't go back, and the giant will
follow Pip away from the camp; Pip: *"Somebody has to tell Dagny."* Nyx names Ilsabet Corrow's banned paper on
copied circles: Halvenreach.
**Cliffhanger (as written):** Karrog told Urzag to bring Pip to the Anvil, and told Vessa the opposite: *"He's letting me go. He wants me to bring her to you."* Then she flinches at a laugh only she can hear.
- The escape, with Urzag's warband in pursuit. Vessa's blessing makes her nearly unstoppable, and Karrog's voice in her head grows louder.
- Distrust: Aldric won't turn his back on a Karrog champion. Nyx sees a mirror of herself.
- Vessa explains Karrog's leash: power keeps flowing as long as you obey. She is disobeying, and it hasn't punished her yet.
- The party is now complete: Aldric, Nyx, Pip, Hask, Brokk, Vessa.
- Decision: go south to Halvenreach to find a spellcrafter who can read Pip's circle.
**Cliffhanger:** Vessa admits why Karrog hasn't punished her: *"He's letting me go. He wants me to bring her to you."*

### 13. The Collegium ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Ilsabet, Edda · **Day 9 → dusk of Day 14, Halvenreach to Cray's Ferry**
*As written:* In the Sealed Archive, the Thornwall's founding papers: the first wardens *copied* the great rings
from the Elder Stone, given them by the Warden himself, which none of them could read; the *Keth* strokes are a
translation from another tongue. The Archive register: Orrin Vell, clerk to the High Chancellor, read the
Thornwall papers 31 days before the Silence. Day 10: twelve crown agents arrive; Brandt reported Edda's well and
Edda is added to the list; Brandt believes Ilsabet and chooses the crown anyway (*"the first chance we've had in
a thousand years"*). Ilsabet is locked in the east tower again. Hale's quiet help (*"I didn't teach you anything
else"*). Edda sets the practice room on fire on purpose, opens Ilsabet's lock with the Lesser Mending, and Tam
Marsh takes them north up the Halven: Ilsabet wants to put her hands on the Thornwall. Ilsabet teaches Edda to
rebuild the Light of the Lantern-Keeper. At Elder Wick, Edda heals all day for free and has to leave thirty
people on the jetty (*"whoever holds the leash on you gets to decide which villages you stop at"*). Cray's Ferry:
Pip to Edda: *"Yours goes all the way down. Where the old writing is."* Ilsabet can't read Pip's circle: *"It's
what Vaelic came from."*
**Cliffhanger (as written):** The *Keth* rings of the rubbing and of Pip's circle match stroke for stroke. The Thornwall was carved from a stone no warden could read, and a nine-year-old is drawing it in the mud from memory.
- Ilsabet refuses the sealed order to go to Solmere; she is placed under house arrest by royal agents.
- Edda helps her escape the tower with Hale's quiet help.
- The two arrive in the river valley just as the party does. Ilsabet sees Pip's circle and recognizes nothing in it, and that is the point: it isn't Vaelic. It's what Vaelic came from.
- Edda and Pip connect immediately. Pip says Edda's well "goes all the way down."
**Cliffhanger:** Ilsabet lays her Thornwall rubbing beside Pip's circle. The *Keth* rings match exactly. The wall and Pip's circle were written by the same hand.

### 14. What the Wall Was Holding ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Ilsabet, Nyx · **Night of Day 14 → dusk of Day 15, Cray's Ferry**
*As written:* Ilsabet, Nyx and Brokk argue *conduit / line / cut* and read Pip's circle as a machine: every
conduit runs inward and down, like the wall's. Nyx sees it: the power comes in from the god at the heart and is
driven *down* through the *Keth* rings. The Thornwall was a binding, a god's power nailed into what it held.
Brokk's nursery tale: the **Unwritten**, the old stuff that never got a name, big as hills, that couldn't be killed.
Hask: the giants' eyes are exactly the wall's blue, and their footsteps hummed like the wall. Ilsabet: *"They're
full of the god who held them."* Brokk cuts one *Keth* ring in stone; it hums downward; only Edda can feed it,
and it reaches the bottom of her well for the first time. Ilsabet shows Aldric the copied order; Aldric fears for
Bram and the thousand. Aldric kneels at dawn again (*"so he remembers what he's for"*). Pip draws a seven-ring
circle in her sleep, stepping up like a stair, with a gold heart: the Sunspire.
**Cliffhanger (as written):** Collum arrives on Bramble with Dagny's boots for Pip, and news: the giant left the river at moonrise on Day 13, ignored the camp and the child, and is walking down the Pale Road toward Solmere, stopping now and then as if waiting. Four or five days.
*(Changed from the outline's messenger hawk: Solmere could not have known first.)*
- Ilsabet, Nyx and Brokk work the problem together: craft, self-taught magic and spellcraft.
- First partial reveal: the Thornwall was a binding written in the first writing. It held the Unwritten, giants from before the gods' war, and Thalen powered it.
- The giant's blue eyes mean Thalen's power is *inside* it.
- Pip draws a new circle in her sleep: the Sunspire, with a gold center.
**Cliffhanger:** A messenger hawk from Solmere carries word: the giant has turned east. It's walking straight for the capital.

### 15. The Spare Prince ✅ *(written)*
**POV:** Corvel, Oren · **Days 4–16, Solmere**
*As written:* Nobody watches the spare. Greaves starts wearing gloves on Day 5; his fingertips are chalk-white
(the Pale Grace; he never catches the winter cough). Brother Anselm the sacristan: a woman in gray has come in by
the chapel's river postern eight times since midsummer and three since the Silence. Day 11, from the organ loft:
Greaves and Wrenna over the kneeling king (moral gray: Greaves loves the king and believes the realm *"rotted under
the light for three hundred years"*); the Pale Grace whitens Haldren's temples and he stands, flat and obedient.
Wrenna looks straight up at the hidden boy. Day 12: Corvel accuses Greaves before the council; Greaves's careful
repetition; the king: *"Listen to the Chancellor, Corvel. He knows best."* Quill privately: *"You aren't mad"*; the
mages at the Sunspire are hostages. The queen believes him and makes him promise to run. Day 13: Oren arrested
for sedition (*"Tell the Chancellor I said so. Twice"*) and whispers *"The study. Tonight. While they're all at the
Sunspire."* Day 15: Bram's thousand arrive; the relay rider reports the giant four days out; Greaves: *"That would
be the nineteenth. The High Dawn,"* with satisfaction. Day 16: nearly two hundred mages gathered at the Sunspire.
**Cliffhanger (as written):** In a false drawer bottom, Greaves's timetable, written a year ago: *Day 1. The Silence... Day 12. The king... Day 15. The mages gathered in Solmere... Day 19. The High Dawn. The Sunspire. The light.* Corvel climbs down the ivy and flees into the city alone.
- Corvel and Oren secretly investigate the Chancellor.
- They discover Greaves has been meeting a Hollow Choir priest for months.
- Corvel confronts the council; no one believes an E-class spare over the High Chancellor.
- The king, desperate, takes a Hollow Choir blessing.
- Oren is arrested on the Chancellor's orders.
**Cliffhanger:** Corvel flees the palace with a single page from the Chancellor's desk: a timetable for the Silence, dated a year ago.

### 16. Memory
**POV:** Tyriel · **Days 1–15, Ithrel**
- In Ithrel, Ione's silence leaves the memory-keepers blind.
- Tyriel goes to the oldest heartstone to find the memory of the northern city, and finds the gap where it was. Someone has been *removing* it, piece by piece, for centuries.
- She finds one surviving fragment: a child with pale gray eyes being carried south on a sled.
**Cliffhanger:** In the fragment, the man pulling the sled looks back over his shoulder, straight at Tyriel, as if he knows she is watching, and says her name.

### 17. Bloodcast
**POV:** Nyx · **Days 15–16**
- On the road to Solmere, Urzag's warband catches the party at a river crossing.
- Hask is badly wounded. Vessa fights Urzag and loses. Aldric is pinned.
- Nyx is empty. She reaches past the bottom again, and this time it works.
- The spell saves them, at a terrible price: glyph-shaped scars burn into her arms, and she loses something she can't name.
**Cliffhanger:** Nyx wakes and can't remember her mother's face. Her bloodcast scars spell a word in the first writing, one that none of them can read.

## Part Four: The Last Dawn

### 18. The March on Solmere
**POV:** Aldric, Corvel · **Day 17**
- The party reaches Solmere ahead of the giant; Corvel finds them at the gate.
- The city is half-ruled by the Chancellor and the Hollow-blessed king.
- The giant is a day away, and the skirls with it. Urzag's warband is on the plain.
- Ilsabet works out Pip's Sunspire circle: at High Dawn, the tower itself is an anchored circle for Aurem. Lit with the first writing, it might let him through.
**Cliffhanger:** Sereth, the eldest Dawnwarden, recognizes Pip on sight: *"I've seen this child before. In the light, when I was a girl. Standing where Aurem should be."*

### 19. The Sunspire
**POV:** Rotating · **Night of Day 18**
- The siege of Solmere through one long night.
- Aldric holds the Sunspire stair. Vessa holds the gate against Urzag. Brokk and Nyx inscribe the tower. Edda holds the circle's power. Corvel and Oren free the city from the Chancellor's guard.
- The giant reaches the walls.
**Cliffhanger:** The circle fails, one ring short. Ilsabet realizes it needs six rings held at once, and no one alive can hold six. Then Edda says, *"I can."*

### 20. The Last Dawn
**POV:** Aldric, Pip · **Dawn of Day 19**
- Edda holds six rings. Pip speaks the center. The Sunspire lights gold.
- Aurem answers, for a few minutes. Gold light pours down the tower for the first time since the Silence. Every good-god blessing in the city flares back to life.
- Aldric raises the Shield of Dawn one more time and holds the stair against the giant.
- The light drives the giant back. Its blue eyes dim, and for a moment it seems to *recognize* the light.
- Sereth catches Aurem's light in her hands one last time.
**Cliffhanger:** The light fades. Every blessing goes dark again. Pip is standing in the center of the circle, and her eyes are gold.

### 21. Sorry
**POV:** Rotating · **Day 19**
- Aurem speaks through Pip for the first time in full sentences.
- **Reveal:** The Bright Court didn't abandon the world. The gods were silenced, bound by their own Accord, rewritten by something they cannot name.
- He can't stay. He's sorry.
- Corvel exposes the Chancellor publicly, with what he has learned to read in people and the timetable. Greaves admits the Hollow Choir promised him the Silence would be brief.
- The giant retreats north, back toward the wall.
**Cliffhanger:** Greaves, in chains, laughs: *"You think the Choir planned this? The Choir was promised freedom too. We were all promised."*

### 22. The Voice Beneath
**POV:** Aldric, Pip · **Days 20–21**
- Aftermath. Sereth dies peacefully, having held the light one last time. Hask is recovering, but not well.
- The party decides they have to go north, beyond the wall, to where the giant came from and where Pip's story starts.
- Nyx and Aldric make a new bargain, this time without conditions.
- Pip sleeps easily for the first time.
**Cliffhanger (book ending):** That night, Pip wakes. The voice in her head is not Aurem's. It is colder, older and amused, and it speaks for the first time: *"Hello, little Oracle. I've been looking for you for a very long time."*
