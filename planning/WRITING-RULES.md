# Writing Rules

> Author's working rules for every book in the library. Not linked from the website.

## 1. Published chapters are set in stone

Once a new chapter is started, every chapter before it is **locked**.

- Before writing a new chapter, reread **every** earlier chapter and build on exactly what they say.
- If something earlier turns out to be inconsistent, awkward or wrong, **do not edit the earlier chapter.**
  Resolve it inside the new chapter instead: explain it, reframe it, or have a character notice it.
- Fixes, rereads and revisions apply **only to the chapter currently being written.**
- Site material that describes earlier chapters (character pages, map notes, the codex) may still be
  updated for new information, but must never contradict what a locked chapter says.

## 2. Chapter length

15–20 printed pages (about 4,500–6,000 words). Occasionally as short as 5 pages or as long as 50.

## 3. Every chapter ends on a cliffhanger

Tied to a major plot point, leading into the next chapter.

## 4. Before publishing a new chapter

- Reread the new chapter for logic, timeline and continuity against every locked chapter.
- Check every narration cue: speakers have voice profiles, sound effects and ambience exist.
- New characters get a spoiler-free page (only what is known at their first appearance), a portrait and a voice.
- New places go on the map.
- Mark the chapter as written in the book outline.
